### Where the marks are won

- **It reads like a real report.** A CFO follows the summary; a DevSecOps lead acts on the findings — one document, two audiences served.
- **Prioritisation by effort, not ego.** Ranking by risk-reduction-per-hour (and defending it) is the senior move the brief rewards. CVSS order alone is the junior one.
- **Stopgaps that ship.** A SIEM query or WAF rule naming the exact field, with a false-positive estimate, beats "add monitoring." A stopgap they deploy today beats a fix landing next quarter.
- **Trust signals.** Stating the disproved decoy and tying every finding to evidence tells the reader the whole report is sound.
