### Where the marks are won

- **PoCs, not prose.** A forged token shown as `header.payload.signature` with the decoded claims beats three paragraphs describing it. Same for the request lines and the XML body.
- **The chain reconciles.** Every hop maps to a real line in the capture (or a named source/token line) and the chain ends at the exfil data. Gaps lose marks.
- **CVSS done properly.** Full vector strings, defended metrics, scope reasoning where it applies. Bare numbers are rejected outright.
- **Impact in their language.** Customers and naira tied to the BoltCash clock — not "high impact."
