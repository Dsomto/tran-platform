### Where the marks are won

- **Coverage with proof.** The source, the tokens, and the XML each hide more than one issue. Finding them all — each tied to an exact line — separates the top band from "found the SQL injection and stopped."
- **The right class, not just "it's bad."** OWASP category **and** CWE id, correct, on every row.
- **The decoy.** A confident, mechanism-level disproof (or a real exploit) of the tempting construct is a deliberate discriminator. Falling for it — flagging a safe construct Critical — costs you.
- **Discipline.** Verbatim evidence, no padding, no merged rows. A catalogue a busy engineer can act on row by row.
