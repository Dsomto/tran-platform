# GRC Advanced 4: Hardening Compiler and Quantified Risk

## Window and scoring

Use the exact opening and deadline shown in the authenticated stage room. No
revision. Recorded defense. 100 points: release integrity and baseline model 10,
control compiler and rollback 25, measurable security and service delta 20,
risk model and portfolio tests 25, decision quality 10, defense 10.

## Controlling execution route

The portable Linux configuration sandbox in the issued archive is the complete
scored host. It requires Python 3.11 or newer, 4 GB RAM, 1 GB free disk, and no
VM, Vagrant, Docker, privileged host changes, OpenSCAP installation, or internet
access after setup. Never apply assessment hardening directly to a personal or
work computer.

Existing Debian, Rocky, Vagrant, Ansible, Lynis, or OpenSCAP work remains
admissible through a compatibility adapter that exports the same plan, state,
test, rollback, and evidence records. Nobody must discard valid work or earn a
hardware advantage.

## Required work

1. Verify and inventory the immutable sandbox baseline, service contract,
   scanner evidence, packages, ports, accounts, permissions, and control state.
2. Build a configuration compiler that converts declarative control intent into
   an ordered plan containing preconditions, exact changes, validation,
   rollback, dependencies, and reason codes.
3. Implement at least eight controls across authentication, privilege, service
   exposure, logging, file permissions, kernel/network policy, and account
   lifecycle. Do not branch on supplied finding IDs or expected answers.
4. Run baseline tests, apply, a zero-change second apply, service tests,
   rollback, baseline-hash verification, reapply, and final tests unattended.
5. Detect the planted scanner false positive and the remediation that conflicts
   with the service contract. Parameterize the control rather than disabling
   the test or accepting the regression.
6. Combine host findings with `vulnerability-scan.csv` in a risk register.
   Implement a tested optimizer that funds exactly three treatments under the
   private budget, models dependencies and uncertainty, and explains every
   deferral.

## Required proof

- Immutable baseline manifest and complete state inventory.
- Versioned control schema, compiler, generated plan, and rollback plan.
- Before/apply/second-apply/rollback/reapply machine-readable results.
- At least eight security deltas with zero unresolved service regressions.
- False-positive disposition and conflict-resolution evidence.
- Risk register, uncertainty calculation, portfolio result, and tested
  deferrals connected to source findings.
- Recorded defense against one control selected by staff from the controls you
  submitted. This selection is made during defense; it is not a missing file.

## Mission interface and handoff

- **You receive:** portable baseline state, service contract, scan evidence,
  public calculation fixtures, private treatment budget, and control family.
- **You build:** a hardening compiler, idempotent application and rollback,
  evidence collection, and quantified treatment selection.
- **You prove:** security improvement and service preservation separately,
  without privileged infrastructure or answer-specific branches.
- **You hand forward:** residual risks, implemented and declined treatments,
  evidence deltas, owners, and accepted limitations for Stage 9.
