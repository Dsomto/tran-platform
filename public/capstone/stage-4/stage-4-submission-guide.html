Stage 4 — Submission Guide & Folder Template

Ubuntu Bridge Initiative · Cybersecurity Internship · Governance & Risk

Read this before you submit. Stage 4 is handed in once, as a single Google Drive folder containing all six deliverables. You do not submit the six tasks one by one on the mission board — those do not reach the graders.

The one rule

Everything goes in ONE folder, submitted at Dashboard → Reports → Stage 4. Six Google Docs in one shared folder. One folder link. One executive summary. One submit.

If you already submitted the tasks individually on the mission board, that is fine — just bundle all six into one folder and submit that folder before the deadline. The folder is the only thing your two graders open.

What your folder must contain — the template

Create six Google Docs, one per deliverable. Use these names so a grader can find each one instantly, and start each from its template (on your Stage 4 board, or in the all-in-one pack):

File name (recommended) Deliverable Start from this template What it is, in one line
01-risk-register Risk register risk-register-template.csv Five real risks, each with likelihood/impact rationale, a concrete control, owner, residual decision, framework IDs, evidence cite
02-breach-notification-letter Article 33 / NDPA letter breach-notification-template.md The 72-hour regulator letter: controller + DPO, affected volume, deadline, nature, consequences, containment, Article 34 decision
03-board-memo Board slide memo (1 page) board-memo-template.md One page: title, three cited numbers, chart, the ask, speaking notes, the three board questions, a tradeoff, the Amaka press line
04-remediation-roadmap 30/60/90 roadmap 30-60-90-roadmap-template.md First 90 days only: concrete actions, owners, naira cost tiers, acceptance criteria, evidence cites, plus a deferral list
05-control-mapping Control mapping control-mapping-skeleton.csv Eight weaknesses from Stages 1–3 mapped to NIST CSF 2.0 + ISO 27001:2022 Annex A + MITRE D3FEND
06-ethics-and-track Ethics stance + track (write in a doc) Your ethics call on the Amaka reveal + your binding SOC / Ethical Hacking / GRC choice, grounded in your programme work

Turn a CSV template into a Google Doc: open the .csv in Google Sheets (or paste the columns into a table in your Doc). Graders accept a table inside a Google Doc, a Google Sheet, or a .docx — as long as every column is there.

The executive summary (you paste this in the submit form, not in the folder)

The board reads this first. Keep it to about 75 words, three sentences a chair would repeat back:

  1. What happened — "Sankofa Digital was compromised in Q2 2024 by…" (name the confirmed finding).
  2. What it costs — the data impact (a conservative, cited affected-record figure) and the regulatory exposure.
  3. What you are asking — the single top action the board must approve first.

Do not bury the answer. If your first sentence is throat-clearing, rewrite it.

How to submit — step by step

  1. Write each deliverable as a Google Doc. Start from its template, replace every [BRACKETED] prompt with your own work, and delete the grey instruction lines before you submit. A template handed in with the brackets still in scores zero for that section.
  2. Make one Google Drive folder. Name it Stage 4 — Your Name.
  3. Put all six Docs inside it. Nothing else needs to be there.
  4. Share the folder for viewing. Right-click the folder → Share → under General access choose Anyone with the link → role Viewer. Copy the folder link.
  5. Test the link. Open it in a private / incognito window. If it does not open without logging in, your graders cannot read it and it will be marked cannot assess. Fix the sharing until it opens.
  6. Submit. Go to Dashboard → Reports → Stage 4, paste the folder link, write your executive summary, and click Submit.
  7. Do not touch the folder after submitting. Do not rename, move, or delete any file — a grader may revisit it, and a changed folder can break your grade.

Pre-submit checklist

The mistakes that cost people marks

  • Submitting the six tasks individually on the mission board. Those do not go to the graders. Use the folder.
  • A restricted folder. If the grader cannot open it, it is cannot assess, not a low score — but you do not want to gamble your grade on a re-share.
  • Leaving the template brackets or instruction text in. That section scores zero.
  • Numbers with no citation. A figure that does not trace to a Stage 0–3 task earns nothing, and an invented one is a fabrication flag.
  • A board memo that runs to three pages. One page is the test.
  • Declaring Amaka guilty as fact. The evidence is strong enough for board action, not clean enough for public certainty. Separate the two.

You did the hard work across Stages 0–3. Submitting it right is the last, easy point to protect. One folder, six Docs, shared for viewing, at Dashboard → Reports → Stage 4. That is the whole job.
