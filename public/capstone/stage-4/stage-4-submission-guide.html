Stage 4 — Submission Guide & Folder Template

Ubuntu Bridge Initiative · Cybersecurity Internship · Governance & Risk

Read this before you submit. Stage 4 is handed in two ways, and only these two: five deliverables go in one Google Drive folder, and the sixth (your ethics stance + track choice) is written in the platform. Nothing is submitted task-by-task on the mission board except that sixth one.

The two places you submit

1. Five documents → one Google Drive folder → Dashboard → Reports → Stage 4. 2. Ethics stance + track selection → typed in the platform → the "Ethics Stance + Binding Track Rationale" task on your Stage 4 board.

That is the whole job. Your Drive folder has five files, not six — the ethics/track one lives in the platform because it also records your binding track choice (SOC / Ethical Hacking / GRC), which is final once graded.

Part 1 — the five documents that go in your folder

Create five Google Docs, one per deliverable. Use these names so a grader finds each instantly, and start each from its template (on your Stage 4 board, or in the all-in-one pack):

File name (recommended) Deliverable Start from this template What it is, in one line
01-risk-register Risk register risk-register-template.csv Five real risks, each with likelihood/impact rationale, a concrete control, owner, residual decision, framework IDs, evidence cite
02-breach-notification-letter Article 33 / NDPA letter breach-notification-template.md The 72-hour regulator letter: controller + DPO, affected volume, deadline, nature, consequences, containment, Article 34 decision
03-board-memo Board slide memo (1 page) board-memo-template.md One page: title, three cited numbers, chart, the ask, speaking notes, the three board questions, a tradeoff, the Amaka press line
04-remediation-roadmap 30/60/90 roadmap 30-60-90-roadmap-template.md First 90 days only: concrete actions, owners, naira cost tiers, acceptance criteria, evidence cites, plus a deferral list
05-control-mapping Control mapping control-mapping-skeleton.csv Eight weaknesses from Stages 1–3 mapped to NIST CSF 2.0 + ISO 27001:2022 Annex A + MITRE D3FEND

Turn a CSV template into a Google Doc: open the .csv in Google Sheets (or paste the columns into a table in your Doc). Graders accept a table inside a Google Doc, a Google Sheet, or a .docx — as long as every column is there.

Part 2 — the ethics stance + track selection (in the platform)

This one is not a file and does not go in the folder. You write it directly in the platform:

  1. Open your Stage 4 board (Enter Stage 4).
  2. Go to the final task, "Ethics Stance + Binding Track Rationale."
  3. Write it in the text box, in two labelled parts, and submit there:
    • Part A — Ethics stance (450–650 words): your call on the Amaka reveal, answering what you tell the journalist, what goes in / stays out of the Article 33 letter, what you tell customers under Article 34, whether her motive changes your judgement, and the harm your own stance creates. Cite 08-front-page-amaka.md, at least one Stage 1–3 artefact, GDPR Article 33 or 34, and one ISC2 Code canon by number.
    • Part B — Binding track rationale (300–450 words): choose one track (SOC / Ethical Hacking / GRC), grounded in the specific Stage 0–3 work that fits you. Cite at least two tasks by stage/number and one external resource you have already started using.
  4. Your track choice is final once graded — pick deliberately.

The executive summary (you paste this in the submit form, not in the folder)

When you submit the folder, the board reads this first. Keep it to about 75 words, three sentences a chair would repeat back:

  1. What happened — "Sankofa Digital was compromised in Q2 2024 by…" (name the confirmed finding).
  2. What it costs — the data impact (a conservative, cited affected-record figure) and the regulatory exposure.
  3. What you are asking — the single top action the board must approve first.

Do not bury the answer. If your first sentence is throat-clearing, rewrite it.

How to submit — step by step

The five documents:

  1. Write each as a Google Doc. Start from its template, replace every [BRACKETED] prompt with your own work, and delete the grey instruction lines — a template handed in with the brackets still in scores zero for that section.
  2. Make one Google Drive folder, named Stage 4 — Your Name, and put the five Docs inside it.
  3. Share the folder for viewing. Right-click → Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer. Copy the folder link.
  4. Test the link in a private / incognito window. If it does not open without logging in, your graders cannot read it and it is marked cannot assess. Fix the sharing until it opens.
  5. Submit the folder. Go to Dashboard → Reports → Stage 4, paste the folder link, write your executive summary, and click Submit.
  6. Do not touch the folder after submitting — do not rename, move, or delete any file.

The ethics stance + track (in the platform):

  1. On your Stage 4 board, open "Ethics Stance + Binding Track Rationale," write Part A and Part B in the box, pick your track, and submit there.

Pre-submit checklist

The mistakes that cost people marks

  • Putting the ethics/track in the folder, or expecting six files. It is five files in the folder; the ethics/track is submitted in the platform.
  • Submitting the five documents as individual mission-board tasks. They go in the folder. Only the ethics/track is submitted as its in-platform task.
  • A restricted folder. If the grader cannot open it, it is cannot assess — do not gamble your grade on a re-share.
  • Leaving the template brackets or instruction text in. That section scores zero.
  • Numbers with no citation. A figure that does not trace to a Stage 0–3 task earns nothing, and an invented one is a fabrication flag.
  • A board memo that runs to three pages. One page is the test.
  • Declaring Amaka guilty as fact. The evidence is strong enough for board action, not clean enough for public certainty. Separate the two.

You did the hard work across Stages 0–3. Submitting it right is the last, easy point to protect. Five Docs in one shared folder at Dashboard → Reports → Stage 4, and the ethics stance + track in the platform. That is it.
