Board leak rocks Sankofa Digital

The Griot Sat Beside The Board

Security chief Amaka Eze named as the trusted insider who allegedly operated the breach persona while steering the response.

By Nkem Afolabi, Investigations Desk

For weeks, Sankofa Digital used one name for the attacker: The Griot. It sounded external, theatrical, safely outside the company walls. That story collapsed this morning.

A leaked board addendum reviewed by The Lagos Ledger says the investigation now points to Amaka Eze, Sankofa's Head of Security, as the person who operated or directed the Griot identity.

The line counsel underlined is blunt: "Strong enough for board action. Not clean enough for public certainty without process." That distinction now defines Sankofa's ethics problem.

The addendum cites a break-glass approval from Amaka's privileged-access queue, a host-87 containment delay that matched a Security leadership exception path, and a Stage 1 phrase once read as a threat: "Let Amaka know the group is sated."

This is it, one investigator wrote. The Griot was not only inside Sankofa. The Griot was sitting in the room where the response was being shaped.

The control owner may have been the control failure.
Trail of hints

What now looks different

  • Stage 0: Amaka reopened the "probably nothing" Q2 ticket.
  • Stage 1: she delivered the staging zip and later signed the board note.
  • Stage 1 task 3: "Let Amaka know" now reads like an internal status signal.
  • Stage 3: host-87 isolation waited for an exception path only leadership could approve.
Motive

Not theft, not innocence

Colleagues say Amaka spent years warning that legacy admin risks were being postponed. The leaked theory is that she forced a visible failure to win prevention funding. Real customer data still appears to have left Sankofa control.

Governance

No clean answer

Naming her now risks due process. Hiding the role risks a cover-up. Reducing this to one rogue insider lets the board dodge failed segregation of duties, privileged-access review, and exception expiry.
