We read your application closely. Something in how you wrote it told us you will show up and do the work. Now it is time to prove us right.

What happens next:

27 May — Onboarding. We email your login details and everything you need to set up your account.
1 June — The programme begins. Stage 0 opens and your first room goes live.

Between now and then, watch this inbox — every step comes here. And clear your schedule for June: this programme is intensive, and the people who finish are the people who treat it that way.

We are glad you are here. See you on the 27th.

Decline email — sent 25 May
Your Operation Root Access application
From: Ubuntu Bridge Initiative <admissions@ubuntubridgeinitiatives.org>
To: {First name} <{email}>
UBI
Ubuntu Bridge Initiative

Hi {First name},

Thank you for applying to Operation Root Access — and for the time and thought you put into your application.

This round we received over 3,000 applications and could offer only around 500 places. After a careful review, we are not able to offer you a place in this cohort.

Please hear this clearly: this is not a judgement of your ability or your potential. With this many strong applicants, we had to make hard choices — and many people we would have loved to take did not make this particular cohort.

If cybersecurity is something you want, do not stop here. Keep building, keep learning — and keep an eye on us. This will not be our last cohort.

Thank you again for applying. We wish you the very best.
— The Ubuntu Bridge Initiative team
